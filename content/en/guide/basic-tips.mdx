# Basic Tips for New Players

Starting out can be overwhelming. Here are some essential tips to help you progress faster.

## Core Tips

- **Focus on fundamentals** — Master the basics before moving to advanced strategies.
- **Check codes regularly** — New codes are released frequently and provide valuable rewards.
- **Join the community** — Connect with other players to learn and share strategies.
- **Stay updated** — Game changes happen often; check back for the latest info.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. Skipping tutorials — The tutorial teaches essential mechanics.
2. Ignoring codes — Free rewards add up over time.
3. Playing alone — Community knowledge accelerates progress.
